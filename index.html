Slab Capture

Guided floor / slab video capture for stitching tests. Records video, sharp keyframe photos and phone sensor logs.

Lay a tape measure on the surface, hold the phone at capture height pointing straight down, read how much tape spans the short side of the preview.

Recording options

How to walk

  1. Hold the phone sideways (landscape), then point it straight down. Keep the level bubble in the green.
  2. Walk slowly in straight lanes. Keep the speed marker in the green.
  3. Pause, then turn slowly at the end of each lane.
  4. Each new lane should overlap the previous one by about half the screen.
  5. Keep your feet and shadow out of view. Don't change height.
  6. Finish by walking back over the start area (closes the loop).
